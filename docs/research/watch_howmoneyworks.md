Instead, it will solidify a **"whale economy"**—businesses will increasingly automate away workers and cater exclusively to asset-owning elites, while maintaining the disenfranchised majority with digital entertainment and subsistence stipends.
* **Supporting Pillars:**
  1. *Microeconomic margin incentive:* Firms have immediate financial incentives to replace human labor with automated systems (proven in contact centers and digital media).

  3. *The "Freemium" structural solution:* The gaming industry’s business model (99% play free, 1% whales generate all profit) provides the blueprint for broader consumer markets.
