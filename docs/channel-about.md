# Counter Ledger 채널 소개 (2026-11-06 게시, 599/1000자, 링크 없음)

```
Is AI really taking the jobs? Is the economy doing what the headlines say?

Counter Ledger checks the popular stories about AI and the economy against the primary sources: Federal Reserve research, payroll data, government working papers. Each video takes one question you might type into a search bar, shows what the data says, and is clear about what it can't say yet.

Every number on screen comes from a named source, listed in the description.

How it's made: paper props on a desk, built and rendered in 3D. No AI-generated video. Narration uses a synthetic voice.

New explainers every week.
```

- 배너: 시안 A(책상 장부), 프로필: CL — `scripts/channel_design.py`로 재생성
- "every week"는 설계 문서의 주 1.5편 목표 기준. 4주차 체크포인트에서 속도를 고치면 이 문장도 고친다.
